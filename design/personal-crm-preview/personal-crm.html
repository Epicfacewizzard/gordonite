    About this concept & backup

    This explores extending Gordonite’s existing person notes with a conversation timeline and follow-ups. All content is fictional and saved only in this browser. No messages are sent and no Gordonite records are changed.

    In a real implementation, person notes would remain the source of truth and follow-ups would use Gordonite’s existing Tasks. Contact import, notifications, a shared calendar, and automatic linking are not implemented here.

    Design direction: useful context, gentle reminders, and your own words. This experiment is separate from the habit-tracker branch and production app.
